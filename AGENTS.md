# Portfolio editing guidance

Keep writing clear, casual, professional, and in first person. Preserve top navigation.

Keep detailed engineering documentation at https://github.com/CodedWithCaffein/Engineering---Projects and link to it.

Do not invent achievements, dates, results, work history, photographs, or credentials. Goals must not appear as completed accomplishments. Confirm the science fair year and official award wording before adding a dated citation.

Do not publish private contact information, precise locations, schedules, account data, or unreviewed photographs. Publish selected web copies with location metadata removed and keep originals backed up elsewhere.

Do not apply the engineering repository’s license here without Michael’s instruction.

Keep visitor-facing pages free of editorial notes, missing-content notices, upload instructions, and statements about what still needs to be filled in. Omit uncertain details instead.
