I’m Michael Polanczyk, a high school student in Missouri interested in mechanical and aerospace engineering. I enjoy designing, programming, building, and figuring out why something works—or why it doesn’t.

## Education

I attend Leopold High School and take PLTW pre-engineering classes at Cape CTC. My school is small, so CTC gives me access to engineering classes and equipment beyond what is available at my high school. I’m also taking dual-credit precalculus through SEMO.

## Robotics

With FTC Team 3665, the LEOPOLD Gear Heads, I handle programming, CAD design in Autodesk Inventor, and much of the manufacturing and 3D printing work. I like connecting a design on a screen to something I can build and use.

## Other interests

Photography gives me another way to experiment and pay attention to detail. I use a Sony a6300 and am developing a collection of selected images.

I’m bilingual in English and Polish, and I’m learning Spanish. Traveling to Poland has also been part of my experience growing up.

## Looking ahead

I’m interested in studying mechanical and aerospace engineering at Missouri S&T. For now, I want to keep improving through hands-on projects, testing, and learning from the parts that don’t go as planned.
