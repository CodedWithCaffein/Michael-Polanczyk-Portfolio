**Aircraft:** My Vortex project involves 3D-printed parts, electronics, assembly, and flight preparation. I also enjoy FPV drones and gliders.

**Structures:** The MoDOT bridge project gave me a set of material and competition limits to design around. I worked through the design, drew the dimensions, and built the bridge within the time and materials available.

**Computer hardware:** I researched and assembled my PC, then upgraded from an Intel Arc A770 to an RTX 3080. I still have the original GPU.
